Account Registration and Privacy Notice

By creating a Curator account, you acknowledge that:

  1. Account Information: Your institutional email address and account information will be processed to create, authenticate, and manage your account.
  2. Certificate of Registration (CoR): If you request to create or join a class server, your Certificate of Registration (CoR) will be processed to verify your enrollment and determine your eligibility for subject-channel access.
  3. CoR Privacy and Retention: Your CoR will only be accessible to the authorized person responsible for verifying your enrollment. The uploaded file will be deleted after the verification decision has been made.
  4. Authorship Accountability: If you choose to post anonymously, Curator will retain your actual identity internally to support accountability and moderation. Other users will see your assigned anonymous identifier rather than your identity.
  5. Authorized Identity Disclosure: The President of your server may view the identity behind an anonymous post within their server when a reason is provided. An Admin may view that identity only for a server identified in an abuse-of-authority report. These actions will be logged, and the affected author will be notified.
  6. Responsible Use: You are expected to use Curator responsibly and follow the rules established for your class server.
