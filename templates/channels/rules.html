CLASS GUIDELINES

Class Rules

This channel explains the rules governing class participation, the authority of each member role, and the consequences of violations.

1. Subject Channel Access

Students may access a subject channel only when their verified Certificate of Registration (CoR) confirms enrollment in that subject for the corresponding server section.

2. Anonymity and Accountability

Anonymous posting changes only how a member's identity is displayed. It does not exempt the member from class rules or institutional policies.

3. Member Roles and Authority

Student

  • Register and join approved class servers.
  • View channels they are authorized to access.
  • Create discussion threads and replies.
  • Report inappropriate content or abuse of authority.

Officer

  • Perform student activities.
  • Manage member subject-channel access.
  • Add forum channels and edit professor details.
  • Post announcements.
  • Hide or remove content.

All officers hold the same authority.

President

  • Perform officer activities.
  • Verify join requests.
  • Assign officer roles.
  • Transfer the President role.
  • Access audit logs for their own server.
  • View discussion authorship only through the authorized process.

Administrator

  • Confirm server creation requests.
  • Manage user accounts.
  • Review abuse reports.
  • Access audit logs.
  • View authorship only in servers named in an abuse report, subject to the required process.

4. Moderation and Consequences

  • Content may be hidden or removed by authorized officers.
  • A reason must be provided to the affected member when content is hidden or removed.
  • Administrators may take action on substantiated abuse reports, including content removal, account suspension, or server deletion.
  • Access to discussion authorship requires an authorized reason, an audit record, and notification to the affected author.

5. Reporting Abuse

Members may report inappropriate content or abuse of authority. Administrators review reports and may take action according to the system's established policies.
