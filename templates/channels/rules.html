CLASS GUIDELINES

Class Rules

These guidelines explain class participation expectations, membership requirements, role-based access, and accountability within Curator.

1. Subject Channel Access

Students may access a subject channel only when their verified Certificate of Registration (CoR) confirms enrollment in that subject for the corresponding server section.

Access is limited to channels authorized for the member's active server membership and enrolled subjects.

2. Anonymity and Accountability

Anonymous posting changes only how a member's identity is displayed. It does not exempt the member from class rules or institutional policies.

Members remain responsible for the content they post, whether displayed anonymously or under their account name.

3. Member Roles and Authority

Each member's permissions depend on their assigned role and active membership status.

Student

  • Maintain an account with accurate registration information.
  • Request to join an available class server.
  • Access only the channels permitted by their membership and enrollment.
  • Participate respectfully in class discussions.

Officer

  • Exercise the permissions granted to active Officers.
  • Create and edit subject-channel information.
  • Provide accurate professor contact and consultation information.

All Officers hold the same authority.

President

  • Exercise the permissions granted to active Officers.
  • Review and decide eligible membership requests.
  • Manage Student and Officer role assignments.
  • Perform the supported President role-transfer process.

Administrator

  • Manage platform-level account and server administration.
  • Review reported concerns according to established procedures.
  • Exercise administrative authority in accordance with system policies.

4. Moderation and Consequences

  • Members must follow class guidelines and applicable institutional policies.
  • Misconduct may be addressed through the appropriate class or institutional procedures.
  • Administrative actions must follow established authorization and accountability procedures.
  • Members should not assume that content removal, account suspension, or authorship disclosure is available unless the application explicitly supports it.

5. Reporting Abuse

Members who experience inappropriate behavior, harassment, or abuse of authority should raise their concerns through the appropriate class or institutional communication channels.

Do not assume that an in-app reporting workflow is available unless it is explicitly provided by the application.

6. Membership and Account Responsibility

  • Keep your account credentials private.
  • Provide accurate enrollment information when required.
  • Do not attempt to access servers or subject channels without authorization.
  • Respect membership and role decisions made through the application.
  • Do not share another member's personal or academic information without permission.
